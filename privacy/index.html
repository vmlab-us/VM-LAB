VM LAB Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

This unified Privacy Policy explains how VM LAB handles information across its websites, apps, contact forms, support pages, and related project pages.

Apps covered by this Privacy Policy

Seconds

  • Analytics: Yes — limited widget activity analytics only
  • Tracking: No
  • Ads: No
  • Accounts: No
  • IDFA: Not used
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking: No

Seconds uses your device system time and local settings to provide the app and widget. To understand whether configured Seconds widgets continue to be serviced by iOS over time, Seconds uses PostHog for limited product analytics.

The Seconds Widget Extension may send a widget_active analytics event no more than once every seven days when Apple WidgetKit requests a widget timeline. This event is a technical indication that WidgetKit has requested the widget timeline; it does not prove that a person viewed or interacted with the widget.

The analytics payload is limited to a randomly generated persistent installation identifier, the event timestamp, the widget family, and a source value identifying the Widget Extension. The installation identifier is used to recognize repeat activity from the same app installation over time. It is not an advertising identifier and is not tied inside Seconds to a name, email address, account, or other contact information.

Seconds does not send an app-open analytics event. It does not send widget colors, visual design choices, user-entered settings, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising identifiers, or data for cross-app advertising or tracking.

PostHog acts as an analytics service provider for this limited widget activity data. VM LAB uses the information only to measure aggregate widget usage, understand whether widgets remain active over time, maintain the app, and improve the product. VM LAB does not sell this data or use it for targeted advertising.

Website and contact forms

When you use VM LAB websites or contact forms, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email, company, inquiry topic, and message.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide support, evaluate business or web project requests, maintain records, prevent spam or abuse, and operate the website.

Analytics and cookies

VM LAB may use analytics tools to understand website traffic and improve user experience. App-specific sections above explain whether and how each app uses analytics.

For Seconds, the limited PostHog analytics described above are used for product analytics only and are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense. We may share information with service providers that help operate websites, process forms, provide analytics, manage email communication, host data, or support business operations. These providers may process information on our behalf for the services they provide.

For Seconds, PostHog receives the limited widget analytics described in the Seconds section above. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including product analytics, support, business operations, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and security. Retention periods may vary by data type and service provider.

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection regarding personal information. You may also have rights relating to certain sharing, targeted advertising, or marketing communications where applicable.

Seconds does not contain an account system and its analytics installation identifier is not tied inside the app to your name or email address. You may still contact VM LAB with privacy questions or requests, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

California privacy notice

If you are a California resident, California privacy laws may provide additional rights regarding your personal information, including rights to know, access, correct, or delete certain information and to opt out of certain sale or sharing where applicable.

VM LAB does not sell Seconds analytics data and does not use Seconds analytics for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children’s privacy

VM LAB does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through its websites or apps in a manner prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

Third-party services and links

VM LAB websites and apps may use or link to third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy practices. Seconds uses PostHog as described above. You can review PostHog’s privacy information at posthog.com/privacy.

International processing

If you access VM LAB services from outside the United States, information may be processed and stored in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from those in your country.

Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other appropriate means when required by law.

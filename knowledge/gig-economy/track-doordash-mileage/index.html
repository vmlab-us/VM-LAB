How to Track DoorDash Mileage and Expenses on iPhone

Record work miles, earnings, and expenses during a Dash. Review your trip and keep an annual mileage report.

This guide is published by VM LAB, the studio behind GIG Income.

Record a Dash from start to finish

  1. When your work driving begins, open GIG Income and tap Start on Home or in Mileage. The app records distance and work time during your GPS trip.
  2. Pause for a personal break, resume when you return to work, and stop when the work trip ends. Review the distance and times before saving.
  3. Log your DoorDash earnings and link the trip to the corresponding income entry. Enter expenses such as fuel, tolls, and parking separately.

Recover a missed trip

If you forgot to start tracking, use manual entry to record the date, time, and miles you can verify from your own records. Add a note explaining how you obtained the distance. If you worked across two apps on one trip, you can link the same trip to more than one income entry. The trip still counts once in overall mileage totals.

Read your shift totals

In an illustrative shift, 58 work miles, $104 in gross earnings and $17 in recorded expenses leave $87 after the expenses entered for that shift. Use the same method when comparing shifts and account for vehicle costs that occur less often.

Keep a yearly copy

Select a year and export a summary or detailed mileage report as PDF or CSV. Review the trip dates, mileage and linked income before saving your copy.

How to Track Amazon Flex Mileage by Block

Log miles and actual work time for each Amazon Flex route, link them to block earnings, and export a yearly report.

This guide is published by VM LAB, the studio behind GIG Income.

Keep the block schedule and actual trip together

  1. Enter the scheduled Amazon Flex block if you use the hours tracker. Keep the planned start and duration as your schedule reference.
  2. When your work driving begins, tap Start in GIG Income. Stop the GPS trip when the work driving ends, then review the miles and actual times.
  3. Enter or verify your actual block earnings and link the trip to the matching Amazon Flex income entry. If you recorded separate work trips for the same block, you can link both to that income entry.

Compare blocks using actual time and miles

A scheduled duration alone cannot tell you how long you worked. For example, an illustrative $96 block with 64 work miles and 3 hours 15 minutes of actual work comes to $1.50 per mile and about $29.54 per hour in gross earnings. Subtract the expenses you recorded before using those figures to assess what you kept.

Fill gaps before exporting

If GPS tracking was missed, enter the trip manually with the date, times and distance you can verify. When you need a copy of your records, select the year and export a summary or detailed report in PDF or CSV.
